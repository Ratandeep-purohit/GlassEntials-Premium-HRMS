Quick Reference Guide

Follow these rules to ensure a clean, error-free import.

Don't Rename Columns

Column headers and Employee IDs must stay exactly as exported.

Use 24-Hour Format

Enter times as HH:MM — e.g. 09:30 or 18:15.

Don't Touch Locked Cells

Cells marked HOLIDAY or WEEKOFF are auto-filled and skipped.

Duplicate Records

Existing records are flagged. You choose to Skip or Update them in the preview step.

Accepted Status Values

● Present Employee was present. Clock In time required.
● Absent Employee was absent. Leave Clock In & Out empty.
● Half Day Employee worked half the day.
● Leave Employee was on approved leave.
